Situation:  	
The department has noticed a significant increase in unpaid claims over the last few months and desires to understand if the demographic information on the unpaid claims will tell us anything about potential reasons for this situation. 

Request:	The executive director’s office would like to see demographic information on all paid and unpaid claims so they can see what kind of background they are coming from and how paid claims differs from the unpaid claims population. Using the data provided, DWS management has asked that you provide them with the following:

1.	The distinct count of eligible claims that received any benefit payment broken out by gender, education, age (as of November 1, 2024), race, and ethnic origin.
2.	The distinct count of eligible claims that did not received any benefit payment broken out by gender, education, age (as of November 1, 2024), race, and ethnic origin.
3.	Describe the major occupational groups that are the most represented in the data and make recommendations as to what management might do because of this information. 
4.	Provide some basic demographics on our veteran population and some possible suggestions for this group.
5.	Provide any other information you think would be helpful, and an analysis of any trends, or other findings that you feel are noteworthy.
